# SSGAUTO GLASS Business Website

## Overview

The SSGAUTO GLASS Business Website is a responsive business website developed for a local automotive glass company. The purpose of the website is to establish an online presence, showcase the company's services, and provide customers with an easy way to learn about the business and make contact.

The project was built with a focus on responsive design, usability, and maintainability while meeting the client's business requirements.

---

## Features

- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Professional landing page showcasing business information
- Service information for customers
- Contact section for customer enquiries
- Fast and lightweight user experience
- Google Ads integration and ongoing website maintenance
- Create a quote that is sent to your email
